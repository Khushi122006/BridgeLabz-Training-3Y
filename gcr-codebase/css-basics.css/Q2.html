About Paris

Paris is the capital city of France and is famous for its art, architecture, fashion and beautiful landmarks.

Famous Places

The Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum and Arc de Triomphe are among the most famous attractions in Paris.

Culture

Paris has a rich cultural heritage with museums, cafes, historical buildings and artistic traditions.

Travel Experience

Walking through the streets of Paris provides a memorable experience filled with history and beautiful scenery.
